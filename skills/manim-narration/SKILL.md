# Manim narration

Apply this guidance when drafting a Manim lesson. Write `narration` as natural spoken English. Use short sentences, ordinary punctuation, and an occasional direct question or invitation to look. Write the intended pronunciations of symbols, quantities and units in words. Keep the mathematical claim, sign and units clear in the scene and any caption as well as in the spoken text.

With Sesame reference narration enabled, CSM uses the selected audio and its exact transcript as speaker context. Phrasing and punctuation can suggest a rhythm. The model may vary the delivery and wording. Give it the words to say without SSML, bracketed stage directions or speaker IDs. Use a short thought per beat with room for pauses. Manim can use the measured speech duration to adjust beat boundaries within the scene duration limit.

Use a process cue for what the learner can see changing. State a specific value, formula or exact mathematical result after that state is visible. Keep the authored motion and its narration aligned so the learner sees the right state when the voice explains it.

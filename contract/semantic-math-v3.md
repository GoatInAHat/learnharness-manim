# Semantic math scene v3

The companion JSON Schema describes input shape. Renderers also enforce the rules below. Plans are UTF-8 JSON (64 KiB maximum) with plain labels and narration utterances. A scene has one graph and five to eight beats over 90–120 integer seconds at exactly 30 fps.

## Graph and line

The domain is an ordered pair of x coordinates. Each expression is at most 64 nodes and eight levels deep, with a root at level one. Only the root may be piecewise. Its breakpoint is interior, the before branch applies to x < at, and the after branch applies at equality. A join passes only when both branches evaluate to exactly the same finite value and first derivative at the breakpoint in binary64 arithmetic. Algebraically equal branches can be rejected if rounding gives different results. All rendered values and slopes must be finite and have magnitude at most 1e6.

Use the usual arithmetic and analytic differentiation rules. The x node is the independent variable, even when its printed name is t. A smooth node with steepness k and input u returns [sigmoid(k(u−1/2))−sigmoid(−k/2)]/[1−2 sigmoid(−k/2)], with exact results 0 at u = 0 and 1 at u = 1. Use a sign-symmetric logistic derivative and apply the chain rule through the input. This is an affine-normalized logistic, without clamping outside [0,1]. Multiplication by a reciprocal expresses scaling or division by a constant.

At each frame P = (a,f(a)) and Q = (a+h,f(a+h)). A nonzero h is always a secant with slope (f(a+h)−f(a))/h, including when Q is visually close to P. A zero h means exact Q=P, no quotient evaluation, and the analytic tangent slope f′(a). At authored keyframes, a nonzero |h| is at least 0.0001. Consecutive nonzero h values cannot change sign. P, Q and any reference must lie within the closed domain at every frame.

## Timeline

Beat zero starts and settles at frame 0. Each later beat starts strictly after the previous start and settles within the beat. A settled-scoped beat can take up to 150 frames to settle. A process-scoped beat can take up to 300 frames. During those frames, interpolate a, h and camera from the preceding keyframe. Use cubic smoothstep u²(3−2u) for the interpolation weight. Interpolate a, h and camera centers linearly with that weight. Interpolate positive viewport width and height in log space. Return the authored targets exactly at settleFrame. Width and height are full extents in graph coordinates. At each target the camera contains P and Q, and in compare it also contains the reference.

Each beat owns [startFrame,nextStartFrame), using 30 × durationSeconds as the final exclusive end. The target display appears at settleFrame. During movement use the preceding display if compatible with the current geometry, and otherwise use points. Each beat has one utterance and an optional narrationScope. The default, settled, requires cueFrame ≥ settleFrame + 12. Process requires startFrame < cueFrame ≤ settleFrame − 12 and a change in the camera center or extent, or in a or h when the preceding display shows the points. A display or reference change alone does not count as motion. The first beat cannot use process. A process utterance and its caption may describe the ongoing movement or ask the learner to watch it. Specific target values, formulas and state claims belong in a settled utterance or its caption. Authors and reviewers enforce this language rule. The validator checks scope, motion and timing. A following settled beat can reuse the same target to state the result. An utterance starts at cueFrame and must finish at least nine frames before the next beat. Process speech can continue after settling. Leave enough room for actual speech. Optional plain captions are eligible from the cue until the end of the beat.

function displays the symbolic function identifier and curve. points adds P and Q. difference adds horizontal and vertical changes. secant adds the secant line and difference quotient. limit displays a nonzero secant and the limit expression. tangent displays the exact derivative and tangent. compare adds a second analytic tangent at the distinct reference coordinate. difference, secant and limit require h ≠ 0. tangent and compare require h = 0. reference appears only for compare.

A compiled frame supplies the frame index, active beat, cue and effective narration scope, display mode, viewport, P, Q, signed h, line kind and slope, and optional reference point/slope. Keep curve, axes and point identities consistent through movement. Sample the graph within its authored domain. Render formula templates with a math typesetter and use plain text rendering for labels and optional captions.

## Comparison audio

A separately authored master WAV is mono, signed 16-bit integer PCM at 16–48 kHz. Time zero is video frame zero. Pad with silence around utterances at their specified cues and through the final frame. No executable content belongs in the plan. The WAV stays separate from the JSON and may be supplied to either local renderer. Renderers can also synthesize each utterance at its cue from the same plan.
